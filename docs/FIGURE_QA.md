# ICLR figure contract and QA record

## Scope

This record covers:

- figure2_seed_resolved_learning_curves
- figure4_budget_dependent_factorial_contrasts

Both figures are generated by analysis/plot_iclr_gap_geometry.R from the
frozen formal q=256 evaluation matrix. No observation is sampled or excluded.

## Figure contract

### Figure 2

- **Core conclusion:** the four gap arms produce large early FID separation,
  followed by contraction and crossings that depend on training seed and NFE.
- **Archetype:** quantitative grid.
- **Evidence hierarchy:** every seed trajectory is primary; A/B are visually
  emphasized; C/D are contextual factorial arms.
- **Replicate unit:** training seed.
- **Repeated measurements:** budget and NFE.
- **Final size:** 139.7 mm by 88 mm, matching the 5.5-inch ICLR text width.
- **Exports:** editable SVG, PDF, and 600-dpi PNG preview.

### Figure 4

- **Core conclusion:** complete, target, denominator, and interaction quality
  contrasts vary across budget and cannot be summarized by a single 256-kimg
  factorial table.
- **Archetype:** quantitative grid.
- **Evidence hierarchy:** the three seed-resolved contrasts are primary; the
  blue cohort mean is descriptive.
- **Replicate unit:** training seed.
- **Center statistic:** arithmetic mean of three within-seed contrasts.
- **Spread:** no interval is overlaid because all three seed trajectories are
  shown directly; the mean is not treated as an additional replicate.
- **Axis transform:** a shared signed pseudo-log transform with sigma = 1,
  linear near zero and logarithmic in both tails.
- **Final size:** 139.7 mm by 92 mm.
- **Exports:** editable SVG, PDF, and 600-dpi PNG preview.

## Manuscript use

Use the PDF files in the ICLR TeX source. SVG files are the editable masters,
and PNG files are previews only. The captions must state the seed unit, the log
or pseudo-log transform, and that the cohort mean is descriptive.
